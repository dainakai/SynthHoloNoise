Model and scope
SHN combines low-frequency variation, row and column bias, a fixed pattern, and temporally correlated fluctuations. Its 44 templates come from 11 acquisition dates, two cameras, and two time segments.
This is an additive model of residuals after background removal. Strength 1 uses the stored coefficients; it does not guarantee the full experimental standard deviation. Signal-dependent shot noise and changes in mean brightness are outside its scope.
